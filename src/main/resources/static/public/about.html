About the institute

Technology-focused higher education.

Makerere Institute of Technology (MIT) is a higher education institution offering programmes in computing, engineering, business and related fields.

Institutional overview

Learning across practical disciplines

The institute’s public programme information includes undergraduate study in computing, engineering, business and related areas. Prospective students can review active programmes published for applications and use the admissions process to submit an application or check its status.

This overview is intentionally limited to information supported by the institute’s available programme and admissions materials. No unverified history, rankings, campus locations, leadership details, vision, mission or motto are stated here.
