Privacy information

The portal processes account, academic, attendance, finance, and service-request data to provide institutional services. Access is restricted by role and student ownership.

Public admissions applicants

The public application asks for your name and email, an optional phone number, and the programme and academic year you select. This information is used to receive and review your application and to let you check its status using your application number and the email used when applying. Admissions applications are available to authorized admissions administrators for review.

If email delivery is configured, the portal emails an application receipt to the address provided. If an application is accepted and the applicant is enrolled, the portal emails the student username and a one-time temporary password to that address. The student must change the temporary password before accessing student services. Applying alone does not create a portal login.

The public form does not request academic documents or payment card details. Do not send sensitive documents or payment information through the public form.

The current public materials do not specify an admissions-data retention period or a dedicated privacy contact. The institute should publish an approved retention schedule and privacy contact before production launch. For general support, call +256 741 902525.
