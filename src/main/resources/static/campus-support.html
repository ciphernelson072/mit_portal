Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How do I reset my password? →

Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your student ID or email, and you'll receive a password reset link via email within minutes.

❓ How do I view my grades? →

Navigate to Academics → Grades to see your transcript and all grades posted by your instructors. Grades are typically posted within 2 weeks of exams.

❓ How do I pay my fees online? →

Go to Finance → Pending Payments, select the payment, and click "Pay Online". You can pay via mobile money, bank transfer, or card. Receipts are automatically generated.

❓ How do I request a transcript? →

Go to Profile → Service Requests, click "Transcript", and fill out the request form. Official transcripts are processed within 2-3 working days.

❓ What's the attendance requirement? →

You can track your attendance in Attendance → Overview. Course attendance requirements are governed by the current institute academic regulations.

❓ How do I join clubs or societies? →

Go to Campus Life → Clubs & Societies and click "Join Club" on any club you're interested in. You'll receive club schedules and information via email.
